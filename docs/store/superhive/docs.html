Install

FlexSpace is a Blender extension for Blender 5.3 or newer.

  1. Download the zip from your Superhive library.
  2. In Blender, open Edit › Preferences › Get Extensions.
  3. Open the menu at the top right and choose Install from Disk..., then pick the zip.

Its settings are in Edit › Preferences › Add-ons › FlexSpace: Modules, Themes, Demo Files and Addon Settings (Appearance, widget animations, Key Hints, the Selection Bar, Update Unmodified Presets, the Shared Presets Folder and the theme library folder).

Your first canvas

Three steps from an empty area to a working slider.

  1. Open an area's Editor Type menu (the first button of the header), pick Flex Space and click New.
  2. Press Tab for Edit Mode and Shift A to add a widget, for example Data › Numbers › Lineal (or just type lineal). Click to place it.
  3. In the sidebar (N), Widget tab, set its Data Path, such as scene.frame_current, or use From Property. Press Tab again and use it.
The canvas is a data-block saved in your .blend file, like a material or a node group. Pick, rename or make one from the selector in the middle of the header.

Edit Mode and Live Mode

Every canvas has two modes. Tab over the canvas switches, or click Edit in the header.

  • Edit Mode builds the layout: add, move, resize, rotate, group and style widgets.
  • Live Mode uses it: buttons run, toggles switch, sliders change their property. Rest the mouse on a widget for a tooltip.
  • On an Armature Picker, Live Mode is Blender's own Pose Mode.
  • Every edit and every use of a widget is one undo step. Switching modes is not a step.
The pill at the bottom of the canvas reminds you of Tab. Turn it off with Key Hints in the add-on preferences.

Add widgets

Shift A in Edit Mode opens the Add Widget panel over the canvas.

  • Search: just start typing. The best match is selected, and Enter adds it.
  • Categories on the left: Buttons, Data, Tools, Graphics, Layout (in a picker, Bone Picker comes first). Tab goes to the next one.
  • Click a card: the new widget follows the mouse, click to place it.
  • The header's Add menu has the same, and the add-on preferences can make it a Pie.

From Property: the quickest way

Shift A › From Property, then right-click any property in Blender's interface. The widget that controls it appears, already bound and labelled. Undo removes it in one step.

Bind properties

Sliders, toggles and property labels control a Blender property through a Data Path, starting from the context, as bpy.context does.

  • Eyedropper: click the button next to Data Path, then right-click the property anywhere in Blender. A slider also gets its Min and Max.
  • Follow or pin: a path starting with active_object drives whatever is active, or always one pinned object. Click the pin control under the field to switch.
  • Copy Full Data Path (Ctrl Shift Alt C) and paste it with Ctrl V.
  • Visible When / Disabled When in the Conditions panel show or grey out a widget while a boolean is on. On a frame, Flex Box or group, everything inside follows.

Animation state and keys

A bound widget shows its property's state in Blender's colours:

  • Animated
  • Key on this frame
  • Driver
  • Changed, not keyed

In Live Mode, over the widget: I inserts a keyframe, Alt I deletes the one on this frame, Shift Alt I clears all, Backspace resets to the default. With Auto Keying on, the end of a drag is keyed in the same undo step.

Pages and modes

One canvas can hold a sculpting page, a weight paint page, one object's controls and an everyday page, and show the right one.

  • Show When (sidebar, Canvas tab, Pages): Always, Mode, Object or Object in Mode.
  • Switch Pages Automatically (on by default) shows the page that fits Blender's mode and the active object.
  • When the page needs another mode, a notice says so: click Enter. For another object, click Select.
  • M in Edit Mode moves the selected widgets to another page.
Changing a page's mode never deletes widgets. An Armature Picker's pages always work in Pose Mode.

Editing tools

Everything here happens in Edit Mode.

  • Select: click, Shift click to add, drag on empty space to box select, A for all.
  • Transform: G R S, or the handles (edges and corners resize, the round knob rotates).
  • Smart snapping: the magnet in the header. Edges, centres, equal gaps and the grid, with guides.
  • Selection Bar: with two or more widgets selected, align, distribute and match sizes.
  • Groups Ctrl G, frames Ctrl J, Flex Boxes, copy and paste Ctrl C Ctrl V.
  • Minimap (picture button in the header) and themes in View › Theme: Auto, Dark, Light or your own.

Color & brush widgets

Edit colours and switch brushes without leaving the canvas.

  • Color Picker: a Wheel or Ring + Square, an old / new strip, and RGB, HSV and Alpha sliders. Esc during a drag puts the colour back.
  • Color Palette: its own colours, or a Blender palette. Click a swatch to use it.
  • Brush Picker: one brush, one click. Works in Sculpt, the paint modes, Sculpt Curves and the Grease Pencil brush modes.
  • Ring Menu: brushes in the outer ring, toggles and operators in the inner ring, the name in the core. Pages hold more brushes; one button fills the ring with every Essentials brush of the canvas page's mode.
The Sculpt Wheel demo has a Ring Menu with every sculpt brush, ready to try.

The Armature Picker

A canvas of buttons that select the bones of your rig. It is saved with the armature data, so it travels when the rig is linked or appended.

  1. Select your rig and enter Pose Mode (Ctrl Tab in the 3D Viewport).
  2. Set an area to Armature Picker and click New Picker.
  3. Select bones in the viewport, click Import Selected Bones and click to place each button. Left and right come together.
  4. Press Tab to pose: click a button to select its bone, Shift click to add.
  • Your Pose Mode keys work over the picker: G R S, I, copy and paste pose...
  • Toolbar (T): Select & Control, Move, Rotate, Scale or Gimball on press.
  • Mirror: moving one of a pair mirrors the other across the red centre line.
  • Bone collections and Bone Names in the sidebar, Armature tab.

Picker with Rigify

A full Humanoid Body picker in one step: select the generated rig, press Shift A in the picker's Edit Mode and choose Bone Picker › Humanoid Body (Rigify).

  • Body: Adult Male or Adult Female, made to measure.
  • Pages: Body, Hands, Feet and Face, each on its own page with its own tab (or side by side on one page with Each on Its Own Page off).
  • Face: every face control on the face art, a Gaze pad drawn as eyes, mouth pads, the Mouth Lock and Eyes Follow knobs, teeth, tongue and the face collections.
  • IK/FK Switches: a puck next to each IK hand and foot.
  • Hands & Feet View: Front (default) or Own.
  • Toes: per-toe shapes need toe chains toe1.01.L to toe5.03.L with limbs.super_finger.
  • Repair Bone Bindings (Utils) after regenerating the rig.
  • Left
  • Right
  • Centre
  • Root
Make the picker for the generated rig (the RIG-... object you animate), not for the metarig. The Body page is a front view: the character's left is on your right.

Presets and sharing

A global preset is a canvas saved outside the .blend, so every project can use it. Presets survive add-on updates.

  • In Edit Mode, click Save as Global... on the top bar. Add it anywhere from the presets menu.
  • A linked canvas shows its state: ✓ Up to date ● Modified ↓ vN available.
  • Overwrite sends your canvas to the preset, Sync takes the preset's, Unlink keeps a plain copy. Restore Previous Version has the last 5.
  • Manage › Export writes a .flexspace file; Manage › Import... adds one.
  • Set a Shared Presets Folder in the add-on preferences to share with your team.

Report a bug

Found something broken? Report it from inside Blender.

  1. Open Help › Report a Bug... in the canvas header (or Report a Bug in the add-on preferences).
  2. Write a title, what happened, the steps to make it happen again and what you expected. FlexSpace adds the Blender and FlexSpace versions, your system and GPU, the canvas, the modules and the last errors of this session.
  3. Click Open on GitHub: a new issue opens in your browser, filled in. Review it and submit it there.

No GitHub account? Click Copy Report and paste it in a comment on the product page or in an email. Nothing is sent by FlexSpace itself, and your home folder in any file path is replaced by ~. You can also open an issue by hand.
